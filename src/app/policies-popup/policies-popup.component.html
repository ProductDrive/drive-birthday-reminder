Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Birthday Wishes Alert ("Birthday Wishes Alert", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on https://www.birthdaywishesalert.co.uk/.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can be used for different purposes, including remembering preferences, providing security, analysing website usage and delivering advertising.

Birthday Wishes Alert currently uses cookies only where they are necessary to provide the service and authenticate users.

2. Cookies We Currently Use

Birthday Wishes Alert uses essential authentication/session technologies. We also use Google reCAPTCHA as a security technology to help protect the service from automated abuse and fraudulent activity. Depending on the configuration of these services, Google may use cookies or other technologies to provide and secure the service.

These cookies are necessary for the operation and security of the service.

They may be used to:

  • Keep you securely signed in
  • Maintain your authenticated session
  • Recognise your authenticated session when you move between pages
  • Protect authenticated areas of the application
  • Support the secure operation of the service

These cookies do not exist for advertising or behavioural tracking purposes.

3. Essential Cookies

Our authentication cookies are considered essential because the Birthday Wishes Alert service requires them to maintain a secure authenticated session.

If these cookies are disabled, certain parts of Birthday Wishes Alert, particularly authenticated account functionality, may not work correctly.

Under applicable UK rules, consent is not generally required for storage or access technologies that are strictly necessary to provide an online service requested by the user. We nevertheless provide this information so that users understand how cookies are used.

4. Cookies We Do Not Currently Use

Birthday Wishes Alert currently does not use cookies for:

  • Advertising
  • Behavioural advertising
  • Cross-site tracking
  • Social media tracking
  • Analytics
  • Marketing
  • Profiling

We also do not currently use third-party analytics cookies.

5. Third-Party Cookies

At present, we do not intentionally use third-party cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking purposes.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy and, where required by law, obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your web browser settings.

However, disabling essential authentication cookies may prevent you from using some or all authenticated features of Birthday Wishes Alert.

Because Birthday Wishes Alert currently uses only essential cookies, we do not currently provide a separate non-essential cookie consent banner.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will implement an appropriate consent mechanism before using them where required.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if:

  • We introduce new functionality
  • We introduce additional cookies or similar technologies
  • Our service providers change
  • Applicable law or regulatory guidance changes

If we introduce non-essential cookies, we will update this policy to explain what they are used for and, where required, obtain your consent before setting them.
